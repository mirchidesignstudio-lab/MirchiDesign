04 · Playground

How to play

01 With your mouse or finger

  1. Drag the globe to spin it any way you like. Let go and it keeps rolling.
  2. Scroll over it to roll it. Hold Shift and scroll to turn it sideways.
  3. RESET brings it back to the front. ☾ DARK / ☀ LIGHT switches the look.

02 With your hand (camera)

  1. Tap ✋ HAND and allow the camera. Your video never leaves your device: the tracking runs in your browser.
  2. ✌ Two fingers up (index + middle) and move them to rotate the globe.
  3. 🤏 Pinch over a button to click it. Pinch and hold on the globe to grab it and turn.
  4. Keep your hand about an arm's length from the camera, in good light.

The camera turns off when you tap ✋ ON again or close this window.
